# Daily Self Evaluation

## 🔥 Take Control of Your Day

Build discipline. Reduce bad habits. See your real progress.

Daily Self Evaluation is a personal self-improvement app that helps you track what you want to do more of, what you want to stop, and how your choices shape your life over time.

If you want more structure, more honesty, and a clearer picture of your habits, this app gives you a simple system to measure your days and improve them step by step.

## 💡 What Is This App?

This app helps you evaluate your day in a practical and motivating way.

You create categories for the important areas of your life, add positive tasks you want to complete, add forbidden actions you want to avoid, and record what really happened each day. The app then turns that into a daily score, a calendar view, and clear statistics.

It is designed for people who want to become more disciplined, more aware, and more consistent.

## 🎯 Why You Need This App

Many people know what they should do, but still struggle with:

- no structure in daily life
- repeating bad habits
- weak consistency
- unclear priorities
- no real way to measure personal progress

This app gives you a system.

- Your good actions are visible.
- Your harmful actions are visible.
- Your progress is measurable.
- Your weak points become clear.
- Your improvement becomes easier to maintain.

Instead of relying on mood or memory, you start relying on daily evidence.

## 🚀 Core Features

### Positive Task Tracking
Create tasks that represent the habits and actions you want to build.

Benefit: You stay focused on what matters and can clearly see whether you are following through.

### Forbidden Action Tracking
Track the behaviors you want to reduce or avoid.

Benefit: You stop ignoring destructive patterns and start confronting them honestly.

### Daily Evaluation
Open a day, enter your counts, and save your evaluation.

Benefit: You get a realistic picture of how your day actually went, not how it felt.

### Weekly and Monthly Goals
Some goals are bigger than one day. The app supports daily, weekly, and monthly task planning.

Benefit: You can track both small routines and longer-term commitments in one place.

### Calendar Visualization
See each day on a calendar with a clear performance result.

Benefit: You can quickly spot strong periods, weak periods, and patterns over time.

### Statistics and Insights
Review charts for daily scores, individual tasks and forbidden actions, and category-based performance.

Benefit: You understand where you improve, where you slip, and what needs attention.

### Category Organization
Group your life into meaningful areas such as health, work, family, learning, or your own custom categories.

Benefit: You do not just track activity, you understand which part of life is getting stronger or weaker.

### Backup and Restore
Export your data as a JSON file and import it again later.

Benefit: Your progress is portable and easier to protect.

### Language Switching
Use the app in English or Arabic and switch from Settings.

Benefit: You can use the app in the language that feels most natural to you.

## 🧠 How the System Works

The system is simple:

- Positive tasks increase your score.
- Forbidden actions decrease your score.
- Each item has an importance level, so not everything affects your score equally.
- Daily tasks are judged for that day.
- Weekly tasks build up across the week.
- Monthly tasks build up across the month.

If you complete more of an important positive task, your score improves more.

If you repeat an important forbidden action, your score drops more.

If you go beyond your target on a positive task, the app rewards that extra effort.

For weekly and monthly tasks, progress is tracked across the full period instead of only one day.

## 📅 How To Use the App

1. Add or review your categories.
2. Create your positive tasks.
3. Create your forbidden actions.
4. Set the importance of each item.
5. Open a day and enter what you completed and what happened.
6. Save the evaluation.
7. Review your calendar results.
8. Open Statistics to study trends for tasks, habits, and categories.

## 📊 Statistics and Insights

The app gives you more than a score. It helps you understand your behavior.

You can review:

- a daily score chart to see your overall direction
- task and forbidden-action charts to see which habits are improving or getting worse
- category analysis to understand which areas of life are helping or hurting your progress

This makes it easier to answer important questions:

- Are you becoming more consistent?
- Which habits are hurting you most?
- Which category of life needs the most work?
- Are your weekly and monthly goals actually improving?

## 💾 Backup and Restore

You can save your data and move it when needed.

- Use Download Memory to export your data as a JSON file.
- Keep that file somewhere safe.
- Use Upload Memory to bring the data back again.

When you restore a backup, the current app data is replaced by the imported data.

## 🌍 Languages

- English is the default language.
- Arabic is fully supported.
- You can switch languages at any time from Settings.

## ⚠️ Important Notes

- Future days cannot be evaluated.
- Today only counts after you save the evaluation.
- Past days can still affect your results even if you did not manually save them, because missed positive tasks are counted for past dates.
- Forbidden actions are not automatically added to past days unless you record them.
- Going beyond a positive target is rewarded.
- Weekly progress depends on language: Arabic weeks start on Saturday, English weeks start on Monday.
- Default categories are protected, while custom categories can be edited or removed.
